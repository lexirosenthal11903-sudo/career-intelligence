Jack & Jill is an AI-powered recruiting marketplace. "Jack" = free AI career agent for job seekers. "Jill" = AI recruiter tool for employers.

They have a fleet of internal AI agents: Juno, Joe, James, Jules, Jessica, Janice.

**Scale (as of June 2026):** 200,000+ users, 60+ PRs/week.

They open-sourced their agent-building framework because AI agent development is relatively unexplored territory. CTO active on LinkedIn sharing guides — likely the source of the LinkedIn post Lexi recalled.
