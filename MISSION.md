# Mission — the why (north star)

_Written 2026-06-23 after Lexi pulled us back to the original purpose. This is the spine. Every feature is
judged against it. If work drifts into mechanics and loses this, re-read it._

## The problem we exist to fix
People are sending **hundreds of applications and hearing nothing back.** It's exhausting and
demoralising, and the job market is changing faster than people can keep up with. This is the acute,
present-day pain — bigger right now than "I don't know what I want."

## What we do about it
Flip spray-and-pray into **fewer applications as a genuinely stronger candidate**:
- **Curate the person** — honest direction, the right roles, at the right level (no wasted applications).
- **Strengthen the application** — tailor the CV to the role (beat the ATS), write the cover letter,
  surface their real angle.
- **Get a foot in the door** — outreach / warm intros (most roles go through referral, not the front door).
- **Diagnose the silence** — tell people *why* they're not hearing back, which no one does, and fix it.
- **Keep them motivated and current** — a companion through a fast-changing market, not a one-off tool.

The mentorship layer (helping people who don't know what they want) is real and stays — but the
candidate-strength / "stop being invisible" loop is the heart of the near-term product.

## Why people hear nothing → the feature that answers it
- ATS auto-rejects a generic CV → **CV tailoring** to the role.
- Applying to everything, including bad-fit roles → **fewer, targeted applications** (honest matching).
- CV doesn't show why-this-person-this-role → **advisor-written CV + cover letter** around their angle.
- Applying above level / off-target → **seniority + relevance** (the pathway, gateway roles).
- Roles filled by referral → **outreach / networking** (warm intros).
- They don't know what's wrong → **"why am I not hearing back?" diagnosis** (paste role + CV → real reasons + fixes). _Likely killer feature — directly on the pain, no one else tells them._

## B2B thesis (direction of travel, not the next build)
"Not hearing back" is two-sided — employers are drowning in low-quality applications too. The same fix
serves both:
- **Universities / careers offices** — their KPI is grads landing jobs; a candidate-strengthening tool is
  exactly their need. Nearest B2B wedge (Phase 4).
- **Employers** — value fewer, genuinely-matched, genuinely-interested candidates.
B2C must prove the candidate-strength loop first. Don't build B2B before Phase 4 — but the thesis is sound.

## The test for any feature
Does it help someone send **fewer, stronger applications and actually get a response / a foot in the
door**? If not, it's probably not the priority right now. (Pairs with the locked "quality over quantity,
no gamification" rules.)
